01 · About

About me

I'm a junior software developer who likes the practical side of software: features that make a real workflow easier, interfaces people can figure out quickly, and systems that keep working when the connection doesn't.

I studied BS Information Technology, majoring in Web Development, at Holy Angel University. Through school and freelance work I built a WordPress business site for Owens Bakery and served as front-end developer and primary debugger for MedFind, a pharmacy system with offline sync.

At Mekeni I joined the ICT software development team as an intern, working on internal business applications in Laravel and AngularJS and on a Flutter mobile app. Day to day that meant building features, integrating existing APIs, processing data, and testing and debugging alongside the team.
